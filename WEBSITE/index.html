    Women in Law Legal Insights

    Founded by Donjeta Feka & Dea Dehari

    About

    Women in Law Legal Insights is a student-led initiative that provides girls with the opportunity to research, write and publish articles on legal topics they are passionate about.

    What to submit

    Contributors are encouraged to submit original articles of 500–1,000 words with no photos, exploring legal developments, landmark cases, contemporary legal issues, gender equality, women’s experiences within the legal profession or any other area of law that interests them.

    Open to everyone

    The initiative is designed to be accessible to writers of all experience levels, meaning no previous legal writing or publication experience is required.

    Review

    All submissions will be reviewed by the Women in Law Society committee before publication to ensure clarity, accuracy and quality.

    Publication

    Once approved, articles will be published through our dedicated platform and shared on LinkedIn, with full credit given to each author.

    Our goal

    Our goal is to create a supportive, collaborative environment where girls can strengthen their legal research, writing and analytical skills, build their professional portfolios and gain confidence in expressing their perspectives on important legal matters.
